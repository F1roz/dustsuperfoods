How to use

Scoop. Mix. Thrive.

Designed to slot into the routine you already have — no blender required (but we love a smoothie).

1

Scoop

One level scoop — 6.48 g of So Fresh & So Green or 6.57 g of Power Up.

2

Mix

Stir or shake into cold water, blend into a smoothie or detox juice. Power Up also loves your morning coffee or tea.

3

Make it daily

Consistency is everything. 30 servings per pouch means a full month of better mornings.

Recipe

The Dust greens smoothie

You'll need

  • 1¼ cups cold water
  • 1 cup kale leaves
  • ¼ avocado
  • ½ banana
  • 1 scoop So Fresh & So Green

Optional add-ins

  • 1 tbsp ground flax (healthy fat)
  • 2 tbsp hemp seeds (extra protein)
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds (fat + fiber)
  • 1 tbsp raw honey (sweetness)
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Blend everything until silky smooth. Pour, sip, glow.

What's inside the blends?
So Fresh & So Green combines 17 organic grasses, vegetables, algae and natural fibers. Power Up combines matcha, guarana, maca, lion's mane and cordyceps mushrooms, an antioxidant fruit & beet blend, and a full B-vitamin complex. See every ingredient and dosage on our ingredients page.
Are your products organic?
Our blends are made with organic ingredients and natural flavorings — with no additives, no preservatives and no artificial flavorings. You'll find the USDA Organic seal on every pouch.
How do I use them?
Add one scoop to water, a smoothie or a detox juice and mix well. Power Up also works stirred into your morning coffee or tea. Each pouch contains 30 servings.
Does Power Up contain caffeine?
Yes — about 53 mg of naturally occurring caffeine per serving (≈20 mg from matcha and ≈33 mg from guarana extract). For comparison, that's roughly half a typical cup of coffee. Avoid it if you're sensitive to caffeine.
Is So Fresh & So Green gluten-free?
Yes. The greens blend is made with gluten-free alkalizing organic grasses, vegetables and algae.
How should I store my pouch?
Reseal the zip pouch after every use and keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture.
How does shipping work?

What is your return policy?
If something isn't right with your order, email us at elizavelardez@dustsuperfoods.com and we'll make it right.
Can I take Dust if I'm pregnant, nursing or on medication?
Please check with your healthcare provider before adding any dietary supplement to your routine.
