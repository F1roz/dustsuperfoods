Our story

Powder 2 the People.

Dust was created in California with one mission: to improve the quality of people's lives and health — through education, community and the best superfood mixes on the planet.

Why organic

Grown for future generations.

Organic food is grown by farmers who use sustainable resources to strengthen the quality of food for future generations. It's free of harmful chemicals, and it fosters a healthier world in harmony with the environment.

We're profoundly convinced the world needs to move toward organic, natural and nutritious products. That's why every Dust powder is built on organic ingredients — chosen to complement your health and beauty.
