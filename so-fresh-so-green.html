Recipe

Greens smoothie

Ingredients

  • 1¼ cups cold water
  • 1 cup kale leaves
  • ¼ avocado
  • ½ banana
  • 1 scoop So Fresh & So Green

Optional add-ins

  • 1 tbsp ground flax seed or flax oil
  • 2 tbsp hemp seeds
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp raw honey
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Blend until smooth and enjoy right away.
